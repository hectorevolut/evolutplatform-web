Run your LSF crew without chasing paperwork.

Evolut is management software for small light steel framing and construction contractors in Portugal and Spain. It keeps the documents, the people and the numbers of every site in one place.

In development. Built by an installer, from the problems of his own company.

What Evolut is being built to handle

  • Contractor document packs

    Insurance, tax and social security clearances, worker files and safety plans, kept current and ready when the main contractor asks for them.

  • Workers and their paperwork

    Contracts, medical fitness, safety training and PPE records for each person, with a warning before anything expires.

  • Cost per site

    Every receipt and invoice tied to the site it belongs to, so you know what each job really cost.

  • Invoicing and payments

    What you have billed, what has been paid and what is still pending, client by client.

Made from a real company

Evolut is developed by Evolut Platform LLC. It grows out of the day-to-day work of a light steel framing installer in Portugal.

Each feature starts as a real problem on site: a main contractor asking for twelve documents by Friday, a fuel receipt with no tax number, a medical exam about to expire.
